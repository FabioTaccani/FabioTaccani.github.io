In this paper, we examine the mode-by-mode extension to the open bosonic
string of the positive relativistic-oscillator polarisation introduced by
Bars, in which the timelike creation and annihilation assignments are
reversed. The resulting kinematical Fock space has a positive-definite inner
product, but two infinite-mode obstructions arise.

First, every nontrivial Lorentz boost mixing the timelike direction with a
spatial direction induces a Bogoliubov transformation whose off-diagonal part
is not Hilbert–Schmidt. By the Shale–Stinespring criterion, the corresponding
oscillator automorphism is therefore not unitarily implementable in the
modified Fock representation.

Second, we prove that the completed positive-mode Virasoro constraint space is
trivial. At fixed momentum, normalisable states can be identified with
Hilbert–Schmidt operators <span class="math-inline"><i>T</i><sub>ψ</sub></span>
from the repolarised timelike Fock space to the spatial Fock space, under which
the Virasoro constraints become intertwining equations. Using compactness we
exclude every nonzero Hilbert–Schmidt solution. Thus, for every real momentum
and every finite dimension <span class="math-inline"><i>D</i> ≥ 2</span>,

Therefore, the modified polarisation achieves positivity of the kinematical
Fock representation at the price of Lorentz non-implementability and a
trivial matter physical state space.
