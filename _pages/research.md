My research interests lie in mathematical and theoretical physics,
particularly in string theory, conformal field theory and related
algebraic structures.

## String Quantization

I am interested in mathematical aspects of covariant string
quantization, with particular emphasis on the interplay between
Hilbert-space structure, Lorentz symmetry and the Virasoro constraints.

My Master's thesis investigates a modified positive-definite Fock
representation of the bosonic string and the obstructions that arise
when attempting to reconcile positivity with Lorentz covariance and
the physical-state constraints.

## Conformal Field Theory and Vertex Operator Algebras

I am also interested in conformal field theory, vertex operator
algebras and representation theory.

My previous work concerns topological defects in two-dimensional
conformal field theories and their relation to algebraic structures
associated with the Monster module.

## Broader interests

More broadly, I am interested in mathematical structures appearing
in quantum field theory and string theory, including representation
theory, operator methods and algebraic approaches to quantum field
theory.
