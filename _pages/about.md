I am completing an M.Sc. in Theoretical Physics at the University of Padua,
following a period of thesis research at Heidelberg University. My work lies
at the interface of **mathematical physics, quantum field theory, string
theory and conformal field theory**.

My Master's thesis, supervised by Johannes Walcher and Roberto Volpato,
studies a modified positive-definite Fock representation of the bosonic
string. The project focuses on the analytic and representation-theoretic
obstructions to reconciling positivity, Lorentz covariance and the Virasoro
constraints.

I have also worked on topological defects in the Monster module, with an
emphasis on modular data, consistency conditions and fusion rules for the
cases \(k=3,5,7\).
