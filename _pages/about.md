## Current research

I study representations of vertex algebras and W-superalgebras, with a focus on
spectral flow and its relation to quantum Hamiltonian reduction. One aim is to use
these methods to construct explicit modules for *N* = 2 superconformal algebras.

[More about my current research](/research/#current-research)

## Previous research

### Bosonic string quantization

For my M.Sc. in Physics at the University of Padua, I studied a modified
positive-definite Fock representation of the bosonic string. The project asks
whether Lorentz transformations can be implemented unitarily and whether
the positive Virasoro modes have a nonzero common kernel. I carried out this
work at Heidelberg and Padua under the supervision of
[Johannes Walcher](https://web.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/physmath/walcher) and
[Roberto Volpato](https://unifind.unipd.it/resource/person/13292?language=en_US).

[Thesis research](/research/#mathematical-structures-in-string-quantization)

### Topological defects in the Monster module

I studied modular transformations of defect and twisted-sector partition
functions and derived the associated fusion rules.

[Monster module project](/research/#topological-defects-in-the-monster-module)
